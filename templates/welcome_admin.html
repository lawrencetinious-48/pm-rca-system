Terms & Conditions

This Preventative Maintenance (PM) Management System supports recording, tracking, and reporting of maintenance activities, incidents, and operational metrics. It is provided and maintained by Infinite Technologies for authorized organizational use.

Definitions

Administrator: the first user created here and any subsequent user assigned the admin role. User: any person with an account in this system.

Authorized use

Access is granted only to authorized personnel. Administrators must assign roles and access rights responsibly and ensure least-privilege practices. Users must not attempt to access accounts, roles, or data they are not authorized to view.

Data protection & ownership

All data entered into this system is the property of the deploying organization. Administrators should define retention and export policies. Infinite Technologies does not retain ownership of customer data.

Security obligations

  • Protect passwords and credentials; do not share accounts.
  • Report suspected compromises immediately to your administrator.
  • Avoid uploading sensitive personal data unless permitted by policy.

Administrator responsibilities

  • Create and manage user accounts and roles.
  • Maintain backups and recovery plans.
  • Enforce internal policy and secure deployment practices.
  • Rotate secrets and require strong passwords.

Liability & warranty

The software is provided "as-is". Infinite Technologies disclaims implied warranties. The deploying organization is responsible for operational configuration, backups, and secure deployment.
